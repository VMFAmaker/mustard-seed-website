1. Application

1.1These Standard Payment Terms apply to all fee-based engagements with Mustard Seed Ltd ("Mustard Seed") unless expressly varied in writing in the relevant service agreement or engagement letter.

1.2These terms supplement the General Terms of Business. Where there is a conflict, the specific service agreement prevails, then these Payment Terms, then the General Terms.

2. Invoicing

2.1Mustard Seed shall issue invoices in accordance with the payment schedule agreed in the relevant service agreement.

2.2Each invoice shall include:

  • (a)Mustard Seed's company name, address, and company number;
  • (b)a unique invoice number;
  • (c)the date of the invoice;
  • (d)a description of the services or deliverables covered;
  • (e)the amount due (broken down by fee and expenses if applicable);
  • (f)VAT (if applicable); and
  • (g)payment instructions (bank details and reference).

2.3Invoices shall be sent by email to the invoice contact nominated by the Client.

3. Payment Terms

3.1Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date ("Due Date").

3.2Payment shall be made by bank transfer to the account specified on the invoice.

3.3Payment is due in pounds sterling (GBP) unless otherwise agreed.

3.4The Client shall make payment without any deduction, set-off, or counterclaim, unless required by law.

4. Value Added Tax

4.1All fees quoted by Mustard Seed are exclusive of VAT.

4.2If Mustard Seed is registered for VAT, VAT shall be added to invoices at the prevailing rate.

4.3Mustard Seed will provide a valid VAT invoice where applicable.

5. Expenses

5.1Where the service agreement provides for reimbursement of expenses, the Client shall reimburse Mustard Seed for reasonable expenses incurred in connection with the engagement.

5.2Expenses shall be pre-approved by the Client unless below the threshold specified in the service agreement.

5.3Mustard Seed shall provide receipts for all expenses claimed.

5.4Expense claims shall be included on the next invoice following the period in which they were incurred.

6. Late Payment

6.1If the Client fails to pay any amount by the Due Date, Mustard Seed reserves the right to:

  • (a)charge interest on the overdue amount at the rate of 8% per annum above the Bank of England base rate, calculated on a daily basis from the Due Date until the date of actual payment, in accordance with the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998;
  • (b)claim reasonable debt recovery costs under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998;
  • (c)suspend the provision of Services until all overdue amounts are paid in full; and
  • (d)require advance payment for future Services.

6.2Interest shall accrue on a compound basis and shall be payable on demand.

7. Disputed Invoices

7.1If the Client disputes any part of an invoice, the Client shall notify Mustard Seed in writing within 10 days of receipt of the invoice, setting out the nature of the dispute in reasonable detail.

7.2The undisputed portion of the invoice shall be paid by the Due Date.

7.3The parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the dispute within 15 Business Days of the Client's notice.

7.4If the dispute is not resolved, it shall be dealt with in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions in the General Terms of Business.

8. Advance Payments and Deposits

8.1Where a service agreement requires an advance payment or deposit, the following terms apply:

  • (a)advance payments are non-refundable unless the engagement is cancelled by Mustard Seed or not commenced for reasons attributable to Mustard Seed;
  • (b)advance payments shall be applied against the first invoice(s) raised; and
  • (c)if the total Fees are less than the advance payment, the balance shall be refunded within 30 days of the engagement completing.

9. Retainer Arrangements

9.1Where a retainer arrangement is agreed:

  • (a)the retainer fee shall be invoiced monthly in advance;
  • (b)unused retainer hours do not roll over to subsequent months unless agreed in writing;
  • (c)additional hours beyond the retainer scope shall be charged at the day rate or hourly rate specified in the service agreement; and
  • (d)either party may terminate the retainer on 30 days' written notice.

10. Equity-Based Engagements

10.1These Payment Terms do not apply to the equity component of equity-for-services engagements, which is governed by the Equity-for-Services Agreement.

10.2Where an equity-based engagement includes a fee-based component (for example, additional paid services beyond the equity scope), these Payment Terms apply to those fees.

10A. Revenue/Profit Share Payments

10A.1Where an equity-for-services engagement includes a Revenue Share obligation (as defined in the Equity-for-Services Agreement), the following terms apply to Revenue Share payments.

10A.2The Revenue Share shall be calculated monthly on the last Business Day of each calendar month, in accordance with the calculation basis agreed in Schedule 1A of the Equity-for-Services Agreement.

10A.3Payment of the Revenue Share shall be made by bank transfer to Mustard Seed's nominated bank account in the second week of the calendar month following the month to which it relates.

10A.4If the Client disputes the Revenue Share calculation, the dispute procedure in clause 7 of these Payment Terms shall apply. The undisputed portion shall be paid by the due date.

10A.5Late payment of the Revenue Share shall attract interest at the rate specified in clause 6.1(a) of these Payment Terms.

11. Records and Audit

11.1Mustard Seed shall maintain records of all time spent and expenses incurred in connection with each engagement for a period of 7 years.

11.2The Client may request a summary of time spent on a particular engagement, and Mustard Seed shall provide it within a reasonable time.

Acknowledgement

These Standard Payment Terms form part of all fee-based engagements with Mustard Seed Ltd. By entering into a service agreement with Mustard Seed, the Client agrees to be bound by these terms.
