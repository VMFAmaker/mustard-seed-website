1. What Are Cookies

1.1Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the website owner.

1.2This Cookie Policy explains what cookies we use on the Mustard Seed Ltd website at [website URL] (the "Website"), why we use them, and how you can manage them.

  • (b)[Any other third-party services, e.g. embedded videos, social media widgets].

4. Managing Cookies

4.1When you first visit the Website, you will be shown a cookie banner asking for your consent to non-essential cookies. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the banner controls.

4.2You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • (a)view what cookies are stored and delete them individually;
  • (b)block third-party cookies;
  • (c)block cookies from specific websites; and
  • (d)block all cookies.

4.3Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of the Website and other websites you visit.

4.4For more information about managing cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.
