1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Mustard Seed Ltd complies with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 in its internal operations.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all directors, employees, contractors, and consultants of Mustard Seed who process personal data in the course of their work.

3. Data Protection Principles

All personal data processed by Mustard Seed must comply with the six data protection principles under Article 5 of the UK GDPR:

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
  • Purpose limitation (collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes)
  • Data minimisation (adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary)
  • Accuracy (kept up to date)
  • Storage limitation (kept no longer than necessary)
  • Integrity and confidentiality (processed securely)

4. Roles and Responsibilities

The Managing Director is the designated Data Protection Lead and is responsible for overseeing compliance.

All personnel are responsible for:

  • Processing personal data only in accordance with this policy and applicable law
  • Reporting any data breach or suspected breach immediately
  • Completing any data protection training required

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Before processing any personal data, personnel must identify the lawful basis under Article 6 of the UK GDPR. The lawful bases most commonly used by Mustard Seed are contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligation, and consent. The specific lawful basis for each type of processing is documented in the Company's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Subject Rights

Mustard Seed will respond to data subject requests within one calendar month. Requests include:

  • Right of access (Subject Access Requests)
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object

All requests should be forwarded to the Managing Director immediately upon receipt.

7. Data Security

Mustard Seed implements the following security measures:

  • Encrypted storage for all devices containing personal data
  • Password protection and multi-factor authentication
  • Access controls (personal data accessible only to those who need it)
  • Secure deletion when data is no longer needed
  • Physical security of paper records (locked storage)

8. Data Breach Procedure

A personal data breach is any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data.

If a breach is discovered or suspected:

  • Report it to the Managing Director immediately
  • The Managing Director will assess the breach within 24 hours
  • If the breach poses a risk to individuals, the ICO must be notified within 72 hours
  • If the breach poses a high risk to individuals, affected data subjects must also be notified

All breaches (including near-misses) are recorded in the Data Breach Register.

9. Data Processing Agreements

Where Mustard Seed processes personal data on behalf of a client (as a data processor), a Data Processing Agreement must be in place before processing begins.

10. International Transfers

Personal data must not be transferred outside the United Kingdom without the approval of the Managing Director and appropriate safeguards being in place.

11. Retention

Personal data must be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Retention periods are set out in the Company's Privacy Policy.

12. Training

All personnel will receive data protection training on induction and annually thereafter.

13. ICO Registration

Mustard Seed is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office. The registration number is [TBC].

14. Review

This policy will be reviewed annually or sooner if required by changes in law or a significant data breach.
