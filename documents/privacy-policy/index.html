2. What Data We Collect

2.1When you visit the Website, we may collect:

  • (a)Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution, time zone setting, and pages visited.
  • (b)Contact form data: If you submit a contact form or enquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), company name (if provided), and the content of your message.
  • (c)Newsletter data: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and name.
  • (d)Cookie data: As described in our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1We use your data for the following purposes:

  • (a)to operate and improve the Website (legitimate interests);
  • (b)to respond to your enquiries (contract/pre-contract);
  • (c)to send you marketing communications if you have opted in (consent);
  • (d)to analyse Website usage and improve user experience (legitimate interests); and
  • (e)to comply with legal obligations (legal obligation).

4. Lawful Basis

4.1We process your data on the following lawful bases under Article 6 of the UK GDPR:

  • (a)Legitimate interests: Website analytics, security, and improvement.
  • (b)Contract / pre-contract: Responding to enquiries about our services.
  • (c)Consent: Marketing communications and non-essential cookies.
  • (d)Legal obligation: Where required by law.

5. Sharing Your Data

5.1We may share your data with:

  • (a)our website hosting provider;
  • (b)Google Analytics (for website analytics);
  • (c)our email service provider (for newsletter communications); and
  • (d)professional advisers where required.

5.2We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

6.1We retain your data for the following periods:

  • (a)Contact form enquiries: 2 years from last contact.
  • (b)Newsletter subscriptions: Until you unsubscribe, plus 6 months.
  • (c)Website analytics data: 26 months.
  • (d)Server logs: 12 months.

7. Your Rights

7.1Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • (a)access the personal data we hold about you;
  • (b)request correction of inaccurate data;
  • (c)request deletion of your data;
  • (d)restrict or object to processing;
  • (e)receive your data in a portable format; and
  • (f)withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).

7.2To exercise any of these rights, contact us at virgilioalmeida2005@gmail.com. We will respond within one calendar month.

8. International Transfers

8.1Some of our third-party service providers (such as Google Analytics) may transfer data outside the United Kingdom. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions).

9. Security

9.1We use SSL/TLS encryption on the Website and implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Children

10.1The Website is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will delete it promptly.

11. Complaints

11.1If you have concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us at virgilioalmeida2005@gmail.com.

11.2You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.
