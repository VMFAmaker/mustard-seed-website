1. Purpose

This policy sets out the measures Mustard Seed Ltd takes to protect its information assets and those of its clients and portfolio companies.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all directors, employees, contractors, and consultants. It covers all information (digital and physical) processed, stored, or transmitted by Mustard Seed.

3. Classification

Information handled by Mustard Seed is classified as follows:

  • Confidential: Client financial data, portfolio company business plans, strategic information, personal data. Access restricted to authorised personnel only.
  • Internal: Mustard Seed operational documents, policies, internal communications. Access limited to Mustard Seed personnel.
  • Public: Marketing materials, published content, general business information. No access restrictions.

4. Access Control

Access to information is granted on a need-to-know basis.

  • Each person receives access only to the information required for their role
  • Access rights are reviewed when a person's role changes or when they leave
  • Shared accounts are prohibited
  • Multi-factor authentication is required for all cloud services and email accounts

5. Device Security

All devices (laptops, phones, tablets) used to access Mustard Seed or client information must:

  • Be protected by a strong password or biometric authentication
  • Have encryption enabled (full disk encryption for laptops)
  • Have automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity
  • Run up-to-date operating systems and security software
  • Not be left unattended in public places

6. Email and Communication

  • Confidential information should not be sent by unencrypted email where possible. Use encrypted file sharing (e.g. password-protected files or secure cloud links) for sensitive documents.
  • Be alert to phishing emails. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown or suspicious sources.
  • Do not use personal email accounts for business correspondence.

7. Cloud Services and Storage

  • Business data must be stored on approved cloud services (currently [e.g. Microsoft 365, Google Workspace]).
  • Data must not be stored on personal cloud accounts (personal Dropbox, Google Drive, etc.).
  • Cloud services must be configured with appropriate access controls and sharing settings.

8. Physical Security

  • Paper documents containing confidential information must be stored in locked cabinets or drawers.
  • Confidential documents must be shredded when no longer needed.
  • Screens should not be visible to unauthorised persons when working in public or shared spaces.

9. Information Barriers (Portfolio Companies)

When Mustard Seed works with multiple portfolio companies, information barriers must be maintained in accordance with the Conflicts of Interest Policy. This includes separate file storage areas, restricted access permissions, and clear labelling of portfolio company data.

10. Incident Response

Any security incident (data breach, lost device, unauthorised access, suspected compromise) must be reported to the Managing Director immediately.

The Managing Director will assess the incident, take containment action, and determine whether notification to the ICO or affected individuals is required under the UK GDPR.

11. Disposal

When devices are decommissioned or data is no longer needed:

  • Digital data must be securely wiped (using approved data destruction software)
  • Hard drives that cannot be wiped must be physically destroyed
  • Paper documents must be shredded

12. Personnel Leaving

When a person leaves Mustard Seed or completes an engagement, their access to all systems and data must be revoked within 24 hours. All Mustard Seed and client data on personal devices must be confirmed deleted.

13. Training

All personnel will receive information security awareness training on induction and annually thereafter.

14. Review

This policy will be reviewed annually or following a significant security incident.
