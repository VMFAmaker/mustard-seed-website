1. Introduction and Scope

1.1These General Terms of Business (the "Terms") govern all engagements between Mustard Seed Ltd ("Mustard Seed", "we", "us", "our") and any client, portfolio company, or counterparty (the "Client", "you", "your") unless expressly varied or superseded by a separate written agreement between the parties.

1.2By engaging Mustard Seed's services, you agree to be bound by these Terms. Where a separate engagement letter, equity-for-services agreement, or service agreement has been signed, the terms of that specific agreement shall prevail to the extent of any inconsistency with these Terms.

1.3Mustard Seed reserves the right to update these Terms from time to time. The latest version will be provided to the Client at the start of any new engagement.

2. Definitions

2.1In these Terms:

“Confidential Information” means any information of a confidential nature disclosed by one party to the other in connection with an engagement, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means.

“Deliverables” means any reports, documents, models, presentations, frameworks, or other work product produced by Mustard Seed in the course of an engagement.

“Engagement” means any diagnostic, consulting, training, advisory, or other service provided by Mustard Seed to the Client.

“Intellectual Property” means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, database rights, design rights, know-how, and all other intellectual property rights, whether registered or unregistered.

“Portfolio Company” or "Network Member" means a company in which Mustard Seed holds or proposes to acquire an equity stake in connection with the equity-for-services model and which has been accepted into the Mustard Seed Business Network.

3. Services

3.1The specific scope, duration, and terms of each Engagement will be set out in a separate engagement letter, proposal, or agreement. These Terms apply to all Engagements unless expressly varied in that specific document.

3.2Mustard Seed will perform all services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with generally accepted professional standards.

3.3Mustard Seed may, with the Client's consent, engage subcontractors or third-party specialists to assist in the delivery of services. Mustard Seed remains responsible for the work of any such subcontractor.

4. Client Obligations

4.1The Client agrees to:

  • (a)provide Mustard Seed with timely access to information, documents, premises, and personnel reasonably necessary for the Engagement;
  • (b)ensure that all information provided to Mustard Seed is accurate and complete to the best of the Client's knowledge;
  • (c)make decisions and provide approvals in a timely manner to avoid delays to the Engagement; and
  • (d)designate a primary point of contact for communication with Mustard Seed.

4.2Mustard Seed shall not be liable for any delay or failure in the delivery of services to the extent caused by the Client's failure to meet its obligations under this clause.

5. Fees and Payment

5.1Fees for each Engagement will be set out in the relevant engagement letter, proposal, or agreement. Where an Engagement is provided on an equity-for-services basis, the consideration includes equity and a recurring Revenue Share as set out in the Equity-for-Services Agreement. No separate fees are payable for core Network services.

5.2Unless otherwise agreed in writing, invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date.

5.3If the Client fails to pay any amount due under these Terms by the due date, Mustard Seed reserves the right to charge interest on the overdue amount at the rate of 8% above the Bank of England base rate per annum (in accordance with the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998) from the due date until the date of actual payment.

5.4All fees are stated exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT). If Mustard Seed becomes registered for VAT, VAT will be added at the prevailing rate.

6.1All Intellectual Property in Mustard Seed's pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, tools, templates, and know-how remains the property of Mustard Seed.

6.2Intellectual Property in Deliverables created specifically for the Client during an Engagement shall be assigned to the Client upon full payment of all fees due (or, in the case of equity-based engagements, upon the relevant equity being issued). Until such time, Mustard Seed retains ownership of the Deliverables.

6.3Mustard Seed retains the right to use general knowledge, skills, experience, ideas, concepts, and techniques acquired during an Engagement for its own purposes, provided that such use does not involve disclosure of the Client's Confidential Information.

7. Confidentiality

7.1Each party agrees to keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and not to disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except:

  • (a)to its professional advisers who are bound by duties of confidentiality;
  • (b)as required by law, regulation, or court order; or
  • (c)where the information is already publicly available through no fault of the receiving party.

7.2The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination of any Engagement for a period of two (2) years.

7.3Where a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement has been signed between the parties, the terms of that NDA shall apply in addition to (and to the extent of any inconsistency, shall prevail over) this clause.

8.1Both parties shall comply with their respective obligations under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

8.2Mustard Seed will process personal data in accordance with its Privacy Policy, a copy of which is available upon request.

8.3Where Mustard Seed processes personal data on behalf of the Client (as a data processor), the parties shall enter into a separate Data Processing Agreement.

9.2Mustard Seed shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special loss, including (without limitation) loss of profit, loss of business, loss of revenue, loss of opportunity, loss of goodwill, or loss of data, howsoever arising.

  • (a)fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
  • (b)death or personal injury caused by negligence; or
  • (c)any other liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law.

9.4Mustard Seed maintains professional indemnity insurance and will provide details of coverage upon reasonable request.

10. Termination

10.1Either party may terminate an Engagement by giving 30 days' written notice to the other party, unless the relevant engagement letter or agreement specifies a different notice period.

10.2Either party may terminate an Engagement immediately by written notice if:

  • (a)the other party commits a material breach of these Terms or the relevant engagement agreement and fails to remedy such breach within 14 days of receiving written notice requiring it to do so;
  • (b)the other party becomes insolvent, enters into administration, or has a winding-up petition presented against it; or
  • (c)the other party ceases or threatens to cease to carry on its business.

10.3Upon termination, the Client shall pay for all services delivered up to the date of termination. For equity-based engagements, the equity earned up to the relevant stage boundary shall be retained by Mustard Seed in accordance with the Equity-for-Services Agreement.

11. Force Majeure

11.1Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under these Terms to the extent that such failure or delay is caused by circumstances beyond the reasonable control of that party (a "Force Majeure Event"), including but not limited to: acts of God, fire, flood, epidemic, pandemic, war, terrorism, civil unrest, government action, industrial action, or failure of telecommunications or power supply.

11.2The affected party shall give prompt written notice to the other party of the Force Majeure Event and shall use reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effects.

12. Non-Solicitation

12.1During the term of any Engagement and for a period of 12 months after its conclusion, neither party shall directly solicit or entice away any employee, contractor, or consultant of the other party who was materially involved in the Engagement, without the prior written consent of the other party.

12.2This clause does not prevent either party from hiring a person who responds to a general advertisement not specifically targeted at the other party's personnel.

13. Dispute Resolution

13.1In the event of any dispute arising out of or in connection with these Terms or any Engagement, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the dispute through good faith negotiation between their respective senior representatives.

13.2If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days of the first written notice of the dispute, either party may refer the dispute to mediation under the Centre for Effective Dispute Resolution (CEDR) Model Mediation Procedure.

13.3If the dispute is not resolved through mediation within 60 days of the mediator's appointment, either party may commence court proceedings.
